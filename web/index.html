The joke needs rules

  1. Stake with a runt. Delegate NEAR to any small validator. It stays your stake, in your account, earning normal staking rewards.
  2. Feed it. Once a round, press Feed. The contract reads your stake straight from the staking pool. The smaller the validator, the bigger your bite.
  3. Make the target. The pack has to feed enough different runts. Hit it and a meal is minted to everyone who fed, and the streak grows.
  4. Miss it and the food spoils. A slice of the Kennel burns, the streak resets and the target falls.
  5. Take the roll call. Anyone can make the contract count NEAR's biggest validators from the protocol's own numbers. If the count rises, the meal triples. If it falls, half the Kennel burns.

What it gives NEAR

A reason to move stake

Delegators earn extra for leaving the biggest validators. That pressure is what raises the number at the top of this page.

A better validator list

Stake, share, uptime and fee in one table, with the small validators first. Your NEAR stays in your own stake.

A decentralization figure with no oracle

Any contract can call nakamoto() or check_roll_call and get a number measured from the protocol itself.

Check it yourself

Live checks against the chain. node scripts/verify.mjs runs a longer list from your own machine.
